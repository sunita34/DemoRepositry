Table 1 : orders (order_id, product_id, customer_id, order_timestamp, order_amount)
Table 2 : product_master(product_id, product_name, product_category)

Queries to write,
1> Query to get total count of unique customers who have purchased product category 'smart phones'

select count(distict o.customer_id) as unique_customers
from orders o 
join product_master pm 
on o. product_id = pm.product_id 
where pm.product_category = ' smart phones';



2> Query to get customer wise total count of orders and total transaction amount

select o.customer_id, 
count(o.order_id) as total_orders,
sum(o.order_amount) as total_transction_amount 
from orders o 
group by o.customer_id;


3> Query to get top 5 product categories by total transaction amount
